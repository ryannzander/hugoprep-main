Hugo Bui

All Subjects & Science Fair Coach · Grades 1-8

I'm Hugo, the founder of HugoPrep. I still remember being stuck on a problem and too nervous to put my hand up, so I try to be the tutor I wanted back then. I teach all core subjects for grades 1 to 8: math, science, English and French. I care way more about kids getting why something works than memorizing it for Friday's test. I also coach UTS entrance prep, gifted program prep, and science fair projects from the first idea to the final board. My own project, an AI system that predicts wildfire smoke, won Gold at the 2026 Toronto Science Fair. Catching up, getting ahead, or prepping for something big, we start wherever your kid is.

FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Common questions from parents and students. Still curious? Just reach out.

What subjects do you tutor?
We cover all core subjects for grades 1-8: math, science, English, and French. On top of that, we offer specialized prep for the UTS entrance exam, gifted program testing, and science fair projects. Hugo covers every subject; Ryan focuses on math and science.
Who are the tutors?
HugoPrep was started by Hugo Bui and Ryan Zander, two Grade 9 students at UTS (one of Canada's top schools) and gold medalists at the 2026 Toronto Science Fair. The team now has eight more tutors, all UTS students who also tutor UTS entrance prep. They're listed in the Tutors section. Because we're current students, we teach the exact curriculum and admissions processes your child is facing right now.
How much does tutoring cost?
A flat $40 per hour for everything: regular tutoring, UTS prep, gifted prep, and science fair coaching all cost the same. The intro consultation is free, and there are no contracts or hidden fees. Book now for a chance to win your first hour free.
Can we do online sessions?
Absolutely. Sessions run online or in person at local libraries, whichever works best for your family's schedule and location.
How do we get started?
Book a free intro on our scheduling page, or send a message using the form below. We'll set up a quick intro chat, learn about your child's goals, match them with the right tutor, and book the first session.
What makes you different from other tutors?
We're top students teaching the exact material we're learning right now, so our knowledge is current and our advice is real. Being close in age also means kids feel comfortable asking questions, and our gold medals and UTS spots prove the approach works.
How long are sessions?
Sessions are typically one hour, which works well for most students. We can adjust the length based on age, attention span, and goals, so just let us know what works best for your family.
